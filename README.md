# Analytical Work
I'm John G. Kathe. This repository is a collection of my work in financial analysis, business strategy, technology governance, and organizational change.

What interests me most is the reasoning behind a business decision. What assumptions must hold for a proposal to succeed? What happens if those assumptions are wrong? And when is it more prudent to delay, revise, or abandon a proposed course of action?

My work generally begins with those questions.
